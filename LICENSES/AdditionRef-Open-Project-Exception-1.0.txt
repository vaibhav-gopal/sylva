- **Combined Work**: a work that combines the Library, or a modified version of it, with an Open Project,

must be provided as the GPL requires.

## 3. Conditions

The permission in section 2 applies only while all of these hold.

(a) **Every part is open.** Every part of the Combined Work other than the Library is licensed under one
or more Open Source Licenses. Its complete corresponding source is available to every recipient of the
Combined Work at no charge, in a way that meets the GPL's section 6 for the Library. If any part is
conveyed or offered only under other terms (a proprietary module, a closed plugin, a paid extension, an
"enterprise edition"), the whole Combined Work is outside this exception, including its open parts.

(b) **No pass-through.** The Open Project is not a Pass-through Work, and it isn't used to give a work that
is not an Open Project access to the Library's functionality. A work that reaches the Library through an
Open Project gets no permission from this exception.

(c) **Each version on its own.** The permission applies to each version of the Open Project while that
version meets these conditions. If a later version is conveyed or offered under terms that aren't Open
Source Licenses, this exception doesn't apply to it. That version may use the Library only under the GPL
alone, the Licensor's royalty-free license where it applies, or a commercial license. Versions already
conveyed under this exception keep it.

network, its corresponding source (the Library's included) is offered to those users on the terms in (a).

(e) **Notices.** The Combined Work's documentation and source carry a notice of three things: that it
includes the Library, that the Library is licensed under the GPL with this exception, and where the
Library's source can be obtained. Nothing in the Combined Work states or implies that the Library is
licensed under the Open Project's licenses.

(f) **Any form of combination.** These conditions apply to every means of combination named in section 1.
Choosing one means over another doesn't take a work outside them.
